ACME FINTECH - INTERIM AI USE POLICY
Version 0.1 (DRAFT)
Effective Date: March 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes interim guidelines for the use of artificial intelligence
systems within Acme Fintech, with specific focus on the Loan AI system used for
automated credit decisions.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all AI systems deployed in production that materially
affect customer outcomes, including but not limited to: credit scoring, loan
approval, fraud detection, and customer service automation.

3. PRINCIPLES
3.1 Human Oversight: All adverse decisions (loan denials) shall be reviewed by
a human credit officer before communication to the applicant. Loan approvals 
above EUR 50,000 shall also receive human review.

3.2 Fairness: AI systems shall not discriminate on the basis of protected
attributes including gender, age, ethnicity, or disability. The Data Science
team is responsible for documenting bias testing methodology and results.

3.3 Transparency: Customers shall be informed when AI is used in decisions
affecting them, in accordance with GDPR Article 22.

4. GOVERNANCE
4.1 The Chief Risk Officer is the ultimate accountable owner for AI risk.
4.2 An AI Governance Lead shall be designated by Q3 2026.
4.3 An AI Governance Committee shall be established with representation from
Risk, Legal, Data Science, and Product Management.

5. GAPS AND ROADMAP
The organization acknowledges the following gaps that will be addressed:
- No formal Data Protection Impact Assessment has been conducted
- No documented bias testing methodology currently exists
- No production monitoring for model drift is in place
- No formal AI risk register has been integrated into enterprise risk management

These gaps will be addressed per the Q2-Q4 2026 remediation roadmap.

6. REVIEW
This interim policy shall be reviewed quarterly until superseded by the
formal AI Governance Policy expected in Q4 2026.
